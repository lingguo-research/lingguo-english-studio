# About Ling

## From Software Consulting to Language Coaching

Welcome to Ling Guo English Studio.

I'm Ling, a language coach and PhD student in Software Engineering based in Montreal, Canada.

My professional journey began in 2013 as a Quality Assurance Engineer at a Chinese software company. In 2016, I moved into software consulting, focusing on software process improvement and international assessment projects.

Through more than eight years of experience in software consulting, I developed a strong understanding of professional communication, analytical thinking, and cross-cultural collaboration. I also had opportunities to support international assessment projects, including interpreting for English-speaking Lead Appraisers.

In 2024, I began my PhD in Software Engineering in Montreal. Alongside my academic work, I started sharing my language-learning experience and helping other learners improve their communication skills.

Today, I bring together my professional background, language-learning experience, and teaching practice to help learners make meaningful progress.

## My Teaching Philosophy

I believe language learning is not simply about memorizing vocabulary or studying grammar. It is about learning to communicate clearly, naturally, and confidently.

My approach is built around three principles:

### 1. Clear and Natural Communication

Pronunciation, stress, rhythm, and intonation all matter. I help learners develop clearer speech and express themselves more naturally.

### 2. Practical and Structured Practice

Learning should connect with real communication needs. Whether you are preparing for an exam, improving workplace communication, or handling everyday conversations, practice should have a clear purpose.

### 3. Personalized Learning

Every learner starts from a different level and has different goals. I focus on identifying individual challenges, providing specific feedback, and building a practical learning plan.

## Programs

I offer several learning options for different goals.

### English Learning

- **English Pronunciation** – Improve pronunciation, word stress, sentence rhythm, and speaking fluency through structured practice.
- **Daily English** – Build confidence in everyday conversations and practical communication.
- **Business English** – Develop communication skills for professional and workplace situations.
- **Academic English** – Strengthen English communication for academic and research contexts.
- **Travel English** – Practise useful expressions and conversations for travelling.

### English Exam Preparation

- **PTE Core Preparation** – Get support with speaking and writing tasks, exam strategies, and targeted practice.
- **PTE Read Aloud Practice** – Develop pronunciation, fluency, and reading accuracy through regular practice.

### French Exam Preparation

- **TCF Canada Preparation** – Get focused support for spoken French, conversation practice, and speaking mock tests.

### Personalized Coaching

I also offer individual learning support, including study planning, progress tracking, and personalized coaching for learners who need a more structured approach.

## Who I Work With

My programs are suitable for:

- Adults who want to communicate more confidently in English.
- Learners who want to improve pronunciation and speaking fluency.
- Candidates preparing for PTE Core or TCF Canada.
- Professionals who need English for workplace communication.
- Students and researchers who want to strengthen their academic English.
- Independent learners who benefit from structured guidance and feedback.

## A Little More About My Journey

Learning a language is a long-term process. I understand the challenges of starting from a different language background, balancing study with everyday responsibilities, and working towards a specific goal.

My own experience learning French in Canada has also reminded me that progress does not happen overnight. Every stage of learning matters.

I hope to help learners make that journey a little clearer, more structured, and more encouraging.
