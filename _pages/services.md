# Software Consulting & Digital Solutions

## Practical Digital Solutions for Small Businesses

We are a Montreal-based software consulting studio helping small and medium-sized businesses turn operational challenges into practical digital solutions.

Led by a software engineering PhD and an experienced project manager, we combine technical expertise with project planning and delivery capabilities.

Clients work directly with a local point of contact throughout the entire project lifecycle—from requirements analysis and solution design to development, testing, delivery, and acceptance.

We focus on practical, reliable, and easy-to-use solutions.
