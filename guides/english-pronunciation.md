## Build Clearer and More Natural English Pronunciation

Clear pronunciation is about more than producing individual sounds. It also involves word stress, sentence rhythm, connected speech, and natural delivery.

LING STUDIO offers two group practice options to help learners improve their pronunciation step by step.

## Two Ways to Practise

### 1. Short Sentence Group

Build a strong pronunciation foundation through focused practice with English words and short sentences.

**Weekly Practice**

Monday to Friday:

- Practise selected English words.
- Listen to and imitate short sentences.
- Record your voice and submit your practice.
- Receive pronunciation feedback and guidance.

**What You Can Improve**

- Individual English sounds.
- Word pronunciation and stress.
- Basic sentence rhythm.
- Pronunciation clarity and confidence.

**Who Is This For?**

- Beginners who want to improve their pronunciation.
- Learners who find individual English sounds difficult.
- People who prefer manageable, step-by-step practice.

### 2. Long Sentence Group

Move beyond individual words and short sentences. Develop more natural English through medium-length and longer sentence practice.

**Daily Practice (Monday to Friday)**

- Receive selected English words and sentences from me each day.
- Listen to the model pronunciation and imitate the words and sentences.
- Record and submit your own voice practice.
- Receive personalized voice feedback and pronunciation guidance from me.

**What You Can Improve**

- Connected speech and linking.
- Word and sentence stress.
- Rhythm and intonation.
- Natural flow when speaking longer sentences.
- Pronunciation clarity and fluency.

**Who Is This For?**

- Learners who already have basic pronunciation skills.
- Intermediate learners who want to sound more natural.
- People who can pronounce individual words but struggle with longer sentences.
- Learners who want to improve fluency through imitation.
