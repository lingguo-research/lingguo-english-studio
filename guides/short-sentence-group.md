## Start Building Clearer English Pronunciation

The Short Sentence Group is designed for learners who want to build a stronger pronunciation foundation through regular, manageable practice.

### Weekly Practice

Monday to Friday:

- Practise selected English words.
- Listen to and imitate short sentences.
- Record your voice and submit your practice.
- Receive pronunciation feedback and guidance.

### What You Can Improve

- Individual English sounds.
- Word stress and sentence rhythm.
- Clearer pronunciation.
- Confidence when speaking short sentences.
