## Prepare for PTE Core with a Clear Study Plan

Preparing for PTE Core requires more than completing practice questions. You need to understand your current level, identify weaknesses, and use your study time effectively.

This personalized coaching service helps you build a structured preparation plan and stay consistent throughout your exam preparation.

### What Is Included?

- Review your current English level and target scores.
- Identify areas that need the most improvement.
- Develop a personalized study schedule.
- Set weekly learning goals and practice priorities.
- Review progress and adjust your study plan when needed.
- Receive guidance and encouragement throughout your preparation.

### A Structured Preparation Process

**Step 1 – Understand Your Starting Point**

Discuss your current performance, target scores, and exam timeline.

**Step 2 – Build Your Study Plan**

Create a practical schedule based on your available time and learning needs.

**Step 3 – Track Your Progress**

Review completed tasks, identify difficulties, and monitor your progress.

**Step 4 – Adjust and Improve**

Refine your study priorities as your skills develop and your exam date approaches.

### Who Is This For?

- PTE Core candidates who need a clear preparation plan.
- Learners who struggle to organize their study time.
- Candidates who want regular progress reviews.
- People who need guidance and accountability to stay consistent.
