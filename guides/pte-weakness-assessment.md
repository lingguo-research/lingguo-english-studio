## Know Your Strengths. Identify What to Improve.

Preparing for PTE Core is more effective when you understand your current strengths and weaknesses. This assessment helps you identify areas that need attention and plan your next steps.

### What You Will Get

- A review of your current PTE Core preparation.
- Identification of skills and areas that need improvement.
- Practical recommendations for your next stage of preparation.
- A clearer direction for your speaking, writing, reading, and listening practice.
