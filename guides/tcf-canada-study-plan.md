## Make Your TCF Canada Preparation More Structured

Preparing for TCF Canada is a long-term learning process. A clear study plan can help you organize your preparation, balance the four language skills, and stay focused on your target results.

This personalized coaching service provides structured planning and ongoing support throughout your French learning journey.

### What Is Included?

- Review your current French level and target NCLC level.
- Discuss your exam timeline and available study time.
- Identify priorities across listening, reading, speaking, and writing.
- Develop a personalized study schedule.
- Set practical weekly learning goals.
- Review progress and adjust your study priorities.
- Receive ongoing guidance and accountability.

### A Structured Preparation Process

**Step 1 – Assess Your Current Level**

Understand your strengths, weaknesses, and target results.

**Step 2 – Create Your Study Plan**

Organize your learning activities around your exam timeline and available time.

**Step 3 – Follow Your Weekly Goals**

Work through planned activities and maintain a consistent study routine.

**Step 4 – Review Your Progress**

Evaluate your development and adjust your preparation strategy when necessary.

### Who Is This For?

- Learners preparing for TCF Canada.
- Candidates working towards a specific NCLC target.
- French learners who need a structured study routine.
- People who want guidance and accountability during preparation.
