## Express Your Ideas with Confidence

Academic English is not only about knowing academic vocabulary. It is also about explaining ideas clearly, participating in discussions, and communicating your thoughts effectively.

### What We Can Practise

- Expressing and explaining ideas.
- Participating in academic discussions.
- Asking and answering questions.
- Preparing and delivering presentations.
- Improving clarity, vocabulary, and sentence structure.

### Flexible, Personalized Lessons

Lessons can be adapted to your study area, academic experience, and communication needs. We can use topics that are relevant to your studies.
