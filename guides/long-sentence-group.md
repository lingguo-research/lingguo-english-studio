## Move Beyond Individual Words

Knowing how to pronounce individual words is only the beginning. Natural English also depends on how sounds connect and how sentences flow.

The Long Sentence Group helps you develop these skills through regular practice.

### Weekly Practice

Monday to Friday:

- Practise selected English words.
- Imitate medium-length and longer sentences.
- Record your voice and submit your practice.
- Receive feedback to help improve your delivery.

### What You Can Improve

- Connected speech and linking.
- Word and sentence stress.
- Rhythm and intonation.
- Natural flow when speaking longer sentences.
- Pronunciation clarity and fluency.
