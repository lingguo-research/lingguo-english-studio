## Make Read Aloud Part of Your Daily Routine

Consistent practice is essential for improving pronunciation, fluency, and confidence in PTE Core Read Aloud.

This group provides a simple weekday practice routine to help you keep practising.

### How It Works

- Monday to Friday: receive one selected Read Aloud practice sentence each day.
- Record your voice and submit your practice.
- Receive feedback on your pronunciation and delivery.
- Build a consistent practice habit throughout the week.

### What You Can Improve

- Pronunciation and word clarity.
- Fluency and speaking rhythm.
- Stress and intonation.
- Confidence when reading longer sentences aloud.

- PTE Core candidates who want regular Read Aloud practice.
