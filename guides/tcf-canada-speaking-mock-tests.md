## Practise the TCF Canada Speaking Test

Knowing French grammar and vocabulary is important, but performing well in a speaking exam also requires preparation, practice, and confidence.

These mock test sessions help you become familiar with the three TCF Canada speaking tasks and practise responding under exam-like conditions.

### What We Practise

- **Task 1 – Guided Interview:** Introduce yourself and answer questions about your background, interests, and everyday life.
- **Task 2 – Interaction:** Ask questions and interact in a given situation.
- **Task 3 – Expressing an Opinion:** Present your point of view, explain your reasons, and develop your arguments.

### What You Can Improve

- Organizing your answers clearly.
- Developing ideas with reasons and examples.
- Using appropriate vocabulary and sentence structures.
- Speaking more fluently and naturally.
- Responding to questions with greater confidence.
- Managing your time during speaking tasks.

### Mock Test and Feedback

You will practise speaking tasks and receive feedback on your performance.

We will identify areas that need improvement and discuss practical strategies for your next practice session.

### Who Is This For?

- Candidates preparing for the TCF Canada speaking test.
- Learners who want to become familiar with the exam format.
- Candidates who need more speaking practice.
- Learners who want feedback before their official examination.
