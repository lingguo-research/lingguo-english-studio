## Build Confidence in PTE Core Speaking

Develop your speaking skills through focused practice, individual feedback, and guidance on areas that affect your performance.

### What We Work On

- Read Aloud: pronunciation, fluency, and natural delivery.
- Repeat Sentence: listening accuracy, memory, pronunciation, and fluency.
- Describe Image: organizing information and expressing key points.
- Respond to a Situation: communicating clearly and appropriately.
- Answer Short Question: listening comprehension and concise responses.
- Speaking fluency, pronunciation, and common performance issues.

### How Lessons Work

Each 50-minute session focuses on your current needs. We practise relevant question types, review your performance, and identify practical ways to improve.
