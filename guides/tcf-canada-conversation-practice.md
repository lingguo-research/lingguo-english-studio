## Build Confidence in Spoken French

Speaking French confidently takes more than memorizing vocabulary and grammar. You need opportunities to express your ideas, develop your answers, and communicate spontaneously.

These one-to-one sessions focus on practical French conversation and help you build the confidence needed for everyday communication and TCF Canada preparation.

### What We Can Practise

- Introducing yourself and talking about your background.
- Expressing opinions and explaining your ideas.
- Describing everyday situations and personal experiences.
- Developing answers with reasons and examples.
- Asking questions and responding naturally.
- Improving vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, and fluency.

### Structured Speaking Practice

Each session is adapted to your current French level and learning goals.

We focus on helping you produce more complete answers, organize your thoughts, and express yourself more naturally.

### Who Is This For?

- Learners preparing for TCF Canada.
- French learners who want to improve speaking fluency.
- Candidates who need more opportunities to practise speaking.
- Learners who understand French but find it difficult to express themselves.

### How Lessons Work

Sessions are conducted through guided conversation and targeted practice. Feedback focuses on language accuracy, vocabulary, sentence structure, and the ability to develop ideas.
