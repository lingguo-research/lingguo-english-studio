## Feel More Confident When Travelling

Travelling becomes easier when you can communicate in everyday situations. Practise useful English expressions and conversations that you may need during your trip.

### What We Can Practise

- At the airport and during check-in.
- Hotel reservations and check-in.
- Ordering food at restaurants and cafés.
- Shopping and asking for directions.
- Transportation and sightseeing.
- Handling common travel questions and unexpected situations.

### Practical Conversation Practice

Lessons focus on realistic situations. You can practise conversations, ask questions, and learn useful expressions for your upcoming trip.
