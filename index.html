ABOUT ME

A software professional turned English coach.

“You don't have to be a native speaker to speak English naturally.”

My professional journey began in 2013 as a Quality Assurance Engineer at a Chinese software company. In 2016, I moved into software consulting, focusing on software process improvement.

As part of my consulting work, I also supported international assessment projects, including interpreting for English-speaking Lead Appraisers. Over eight years in the software industry, I developed strong experience in professional communication and cross-cultural collaboration.

In 2024, I began my PhD in Software Engineering in Montreal, Canada. Alongside my academic studies, I teach English part-time, helping learners improve pronunciation, speaking fluency, and confidence in real-life communication.
